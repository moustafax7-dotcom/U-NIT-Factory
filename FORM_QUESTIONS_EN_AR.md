Use two matching forms if you want the visitor's chosen website language to control the Google Form language as well.

### Important settings

- Do **not** require Google sign-in; avoid file-upload questions.
- Do **not** enable 'Limit to 1 response' (it requires Google login in many cases).
- Publish and allow anyone with the link to respond.
- On the father's Google account, turn on `Responses > More > Get email notifications for new responses` for BOTH forms.
- Link responses to Google Sheets for a searchable log.
- Do not invent prices, minimum order quantities or delivery promises. Number ranges can be asked as estimation, but are **not** minimum order policy.
